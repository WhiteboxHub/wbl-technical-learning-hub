## 2. In-Class Controller Commands

Instructors can type or speak these commands to control the AI Teaching Co-Pilot:
- `next`: Advance to the next beat.
- `back`: Step back to the previous beat.
- `repeat`: Re-explain the current concept with an alternate analogy.
- `answer`: Reveal the model answer and reasoning for the beat's question.
- `more`: Deep dive into memory layouts, strides, or statistical distributions.
- `jump <part.beat>`: Jump directly to any beat (e.g. `jump 1.2` or `jump 2.3`).
- `status`: Show completed vs pending beats in `.class/progress.md`.
- `end`: Conclude session, output summary statistics, and assign student homework.
