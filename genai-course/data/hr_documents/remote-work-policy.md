# Meridian Solutions — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines guidelines, expectations, and security standards for employees working remotely or in a hybrid model at Meridian Solutions.

## 2. Eligibility & Models
Employees are eligible for remote or hybrid work upon successful completion of their 90-day onboarding period, subject to role requirements and manager approval.

- **Hybrid Model (Default)**: Employees within a 40-mile radius of a Meridian office work a hybrid schedule with a minimum of 2 designated in-office days per week (Tuesdays and Thursdays are company-wide collaboration days).
- **Full Remote Model**: Approved on a case-by-case basis for roles designated as remote-eligible or for employees residing beyond 40 miles from any Meridian office.

## 3. Home Office Setup & Equipment
To support an effective home workspace, Meridian provides:
- **Standard Hardware**: Company-issued MacBook Pro or Lenovo ThinkPad, dual monitors, keyboard, mouse, and USB-C dock.
- **Home Office Stipend**: A one-time, tax-free stipend of $500 on your second paycheck to help set up an ergonomic desk and chair.
- **Internet Expense**: Full remote employees receive a monthly reimbursement of $50 toward high-speed internet service.

## 4. Availability & Working Hours
- **Core Hours**: Remote employees must remain reachable on Slack and Google Meet during core team hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their primary team's time zone).
- **Time Zone Consistency**: Employees must reside within 3 time zones of their primary engineering or management team to prevent asynchronous bottlenecks.
- **Meeting Presence**: Video cameras are encouraged during one-on-one sessions, team stand-ups, and sprint retrospectives, but optional during large company all-hands.

## 5. Security & Data Protection for Remote Work
- **Virtual Private Network (VPN)**: Employees must connect to the corporate WireGuard VPN whenever accessing internal APIs, staging databases, or admin portals.
- **Public Wi-Fi Restrictions**: Working from public Wi-Fi networks (airports, cafes, hotels) without an active Meridian VPN session is strictly prohibited.
- **Device Security**: Meridian hardware must never be used by friends, family, or unauthorized third parties. Auto-lock must be configured for 5 minutes of inactivity.
- **Physical Privacy**: Employees must ensure their screens are not visible to unauthorized individuals when handling sensitive customer data or internal source code.
