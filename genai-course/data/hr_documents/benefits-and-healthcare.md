# Meridian Solutions — Benefits and Healthcare Guide

## 1. Health, Dental & Vision Coverage
Meridian Solutions offers comprehensive health insurance for all full-time employees and their eligible dependents starting on the first day of the month following their hire date.

### Medical Plans
1. **BlueShield Premier HMO**: $20 copay for primary care, $40 specialist. In-network coverage only. Meridian covers 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums.
2. **BlueShield Choice PPO**: $500 individual / $1,000 family deductible. Out-of-network flexibility with 80/20 coinsurance. Meridian covers 85% of employee premiums.
3. **BlueShield High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA**: $1,600 individual / $3,200 family deductible. Paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). Meridian contributes $1,000 annually for individuals and $2,000 for families directly into your HSA.

### Dental & Vision
- **Delta Dental PPO**: Covers 100% of preventive care, 80% of basic procedures, 50% of major services up to $2,000 annually per covered individual.
- **VSP Vision Care**: $10 copay for annual eye exams, $200 annual frame allowance, and covered prescription lenses every 12 months.

## 2. Retirement & 401(k) Plan
- **Provider**: Fidelity Investments.
- **Company Match**: Meridian matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible base salary.
- **Vesting Schedule**: Safe Harbor match with immediate 100% vesting upon contribution.
- **Plan Types**: Both Traditional Pre-Tax 401(k) and Roth 401(k) options are available.

## 3. Life & Disability Insurance
- **Basic Life & AD&D**: Fully employer-paid policy providing 2x annual base salary (up to a $500,000 maximum).
- **Short-Term Disability (STD)**: Covers 60% of base salary up to $1,500/week after a 7-day elimination period, up to 12 weeks.
- **Long-Term Disability (LTD)**: Covers 60% of base salary up to $6,000/month after 90 days of continuous disability.

## 4. Wellness & Professional Development Stipends
- **Wellness & Gym Stipend**: $50 per month reimbursed for gym memberships, fitness classes, wellness apps, or ergonomic home equipment.
- **Professional Development Budget**: $2,000 per calendar year per employee for approved courses, technical certifications, books, and conference attendance.
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**: Confidential counseling (up to 6 free sessions per topic), legal advisory, and financial consultation through ComPsych.

## 5. Open Enrollment
Open enrollment occurs annually from November 1 to November 21. Plan changes become effective January 1 of the following calendar year. Outside this window, changes can only be made within 30 days of a Qualifying Life Event (marriage, birth, divorce, loss of other coverage).
