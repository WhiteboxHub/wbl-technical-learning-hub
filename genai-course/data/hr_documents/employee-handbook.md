# Meridian Solutions — Employee Handbook

## 1. Company Overview & Culture
Welcome to Meridian Solutions. Founded in 2018, Meridian Solutions provides cloud-native enterprise intelligence and workflow automation software. Our core values are customer trust, radical transparency, technical rigor, and psychological safety. Every team member is empowered to make decisions in the best interest of our customers and colleagues.

## 2. Code of Conduct & Workplace Expectations
Meridian is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and inclusive working environment free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. 

- **Professionalism**: We expect open communication, constructive debate, and mutual respect across all departments.
- **Harassment-Free Workplace**: Zero tolerance for harassment based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or veteran status. Any violation should be reported immediately to People Operations.
- **Conflicts of Interest**: Employees must disclose any outside commercial activities, advisory roles, or board seats that could create a conflict of interest with Meridian's business.

## 3. Working Hours & Core Collaboration Hours
While Meridian embraces flexible working arrangements, team alignment is critical for cross-functional collaboration.
- **Standard Workweek**: Full-time employees work a 40-hour workweek, Monday through Friday.
- **Core Hours**: All employees are expected to be available for meetings and synchronous collaboration between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their designated team time zone.
- **Overtime**: Non-exempt employees must obtain manager approval prior to working overtime. Exempt employees are salaried and manage their schedules to achieve project goals.

## 4. Performance Reviews & Growth
We conduct semi-annual performance and compensation reviews:
- **Mid-Year Check-in (June)**: Qualitative 360-degree feedback focused on career development, goal progress, and peer feedback.
- **Annual Review (December)**: Formal evaluation evaluating achievement against OKRs, technical impact, company values alignment, and compensation adjustments.

## 5. Disciplinary Process
When performance or conduct issues arise, Meridian utilizes a fair, progressive disciplinary procedure:
1. **Verbal Counseling**: Informal discussion between employee and manager identifying the gap and agreed timeline.
2. **Written Warning**: Formal memorandum outlining the deficiency, required outcomes, and a 30-day checkpoint.
3. **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**: A structured 30-to-60 day plan with weekly milestone reviews. Failure to meet PIP targets results in termination of employment.
4. **Immediate Dismissal**: Gross misconduct (theft, fraud, data breach, harassment) results in immediate termination without progressive steps.
