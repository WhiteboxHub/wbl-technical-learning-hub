# Meridian Solutions — Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policy

## 1. PTO Accrual Schedule
Meridian provides combined Paid Time Off (PTO) covering vacation, personal days, and wellness time. PTO accrues semi-monthly starting from your first day of employment.

- **0 to 2 Years of Service**: 15 days (120 hours) per calendar year, accrued at 5.0 hours per pay period.
- **3 to 5 Years of Service**: 20 days (160 hours) per calendar year, accrued at 6.67 hours per pay period.
- **6+ Years of Service**: 25 days (200 hours) per calendar year, accrued at 8.33 hours per pay period.

## 2. PTO Carry-Over & Payout
- **Annual Carry-Over**: Up to 10 days (80 hours) of unused accrued PTO can be rolled over into the following calendar year. Any accrued PTO exceeding 10 days on December 31st will be forfeited.
- **Payout Upon Departure**: Accrued, unused PTO is paid out in full on the final paycheck upon separation of employment, in accordance with applicable state laws.

## 3. Sick Leave & Medical Appointments
Meridian provides 10 dedicated paid Sick Days (80 hours) per year, allocated on January 1st (prorated for mid-year hires). Sick leave does not accrue and is separate from PTO.
- Sick leave covers employee illness, injury, doctor visits, and caring for an immediate family member.
- Absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days require a medical note from a licensed healthcare provider submitted to People Operations.

## 4. Parental Leave Policy
Meridian offers comprehensive paid parental leave for new parents following birth, adoption, or foster placement:
- **Primary Caregiver Leave**: 16 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first 12 months.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave**: 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first 12 months.
- Benefits and health insurance coverage remain fully active during parental leave.

## 5. Bereavement & Civic Leave
- **Bereavement**: 5 consecutive paid business days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling); 3 paid days for extended family members (grandparent, aunt/uncle, in-laws).
- **Jury Duty & Voting**: Up to 2 weeks of paid jury duty leave. Up to 2 hours of paid time off to vote on official election days if polls cannot be accessed outside work hours.

## 6. How to Request Leave
All planned PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted through the Meridian HR Portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your direct manager.
